01 — About endure

Recovery that holds up under load.

endure is a modern, recovery-focused physiotherapy practice in NewGiza. We help people recover from pain, injuries and movement limitations — and then keep going, with strength and mobility that lasts.

Every plan starts with a full movement assessment and is built for your body, your sport and your day-to-day. Treating the pain is the starting point. Building resilience is the goal.

03 — Services

What we treat.

Five core programs, all delivered one-to-one and adjusted session by session as you progress.

01

Physiotherapy

Hands-on assessment and manual therapy for musculoskeletal complaints, from stiff necks to post-surgical joints.
02

Rehabilitation

Structured, progressive loading programs that rebuild strength and confidence after injury or surgery.
03

Pain management

Practical strategies for persistent and recurring pain — reducing symptoms while restoring normal movement.
04

Injury recovery

Sports and everyday injuries, from acute care to a clear return-to-training or return-to-work plan.
05

Movement rehabilitation

Correcting movement limitations and imbalances so the way you move stops causing the problem.
